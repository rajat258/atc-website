# Content still needed from ATC Group

Everything on the site today is drawn from the two profile decks and the
company LinkedIn page. Nothing has been invented. These are the gaps that
need the client's input before launch.

## 1. Certifications — the biggest gap

**Nothing on the site currently claims any certification, deliberately.**

The profile decks name no certificate at all. The "Accreditations & Awards"
panel in the amber deck contains generic template icons, not real marks, so
none of it was usable.

Every competitor leads with certification, so this is the single highest-value
thing to add. Please send, for each one held:

- The standard and version (for example ISO 9001:2015, not "ISO 9001")
- The issuing body
- The certificate number
- The scope, and which unit it covers
- The valid-to date
- A PDF

Likely relevant in this market: ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 22000,
BRCGS Packaging Materials, SEDEX, Halal, Kosher, UN certification for
dangerous goods, and any export credential such as AEO or Export House.

Worth knowing: two of the three competitors are currently citing **withdrawn
or superseded** standards on their own sites. Publishing current certificates
with numbers and expiry dates is a genuine advantage, not just table stakes.

## 2. Address

The decks say only "Narol, Gujarat" and "the outskirts of Ahmedabad". LinkedIn
gives the pin code 382405.

Needed: the full postal address of each of the three units, plus which one
visitors and couriers should use. A Google Maps link would let us embed a map
on the contact page.

## 3. Photography

The site currently uses the client's own WhatsApp photographs. They are honest
and they work, but several are phone snaps of a sunset rather than of a bag.

Worth commissioning, in rough priority order:

1. Individual bag types shot cleanly against a plain ground. This matters most.
   A buyer comparing a U panel to a baffle bag cannot do it from a photo of a
   stack in a yard.
2. The clean room or segregated packing area. This is a selling point that is
   currently described but not shown.
3. Looms and the extrusion line running.
4. A dispatch shot with a loaded container.

## 4. Numbers worth confirming

Currently on the site, all taken from the decks:

- 2.5 million bags a year, across three units
- More than 10 states served domestically
- Akshay Trading Co. trading since 1988
- Akshay FIBC manufacturing since 1993

Note the decks say "35 years" alongside a 1988 founding date, which was
written around 2023. The site calculates the figure from the founding year at
runtime instead, so it will not go stale again.

Still missing, and all of them would strengthen the site:

- Plant area in square feet
- Number of looms, stitching machines, extrusion lines
- Employee count
- Named export markets. Competitors list theirs country by country.
- Monthly polymer conversion in MT

## 5. Specification ranges to verify

The product specification tables describe standard FIBC industry ranges. A
production person should confirm each line against what the plant actually
builds:

- Safe working load 500 kg to 2,000 kg
- Safety factor 5:1 single trip, 6:1 multi trip
- Base 75 x 75 cm to 115 x 115 cm, height 75 cm to 200 cm
- Up to four colour printing
- UV stabilisation level, ideally as a KLY figure and the test standard

Anything the plant does not do should come off the site.

## 6. Nice to have

- A downloadable PDF datasheet per bag type. No competitor offers one.
- A safe handling guide and a food safety guide as PDFs.
- Named customers or case studies, if any client will agree to be named.
- Minimum order quantity and typical lead time. Nobody in this market
  publishes either, so doing it would stand out.
- A form backend. The enquiry form currently opens the visitor's email client.
  A service such as Formspree or a small serverless function would capture
  submissions properly and stop losing anyone whose browser has no mail client
  configured.
